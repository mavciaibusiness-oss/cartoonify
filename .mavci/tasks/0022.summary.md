## What was built

The spec's design, in summary (details in the spec):

- Sign-in before conversion: Google, or an e-mail link with no password. Routes under `/api/auth/*`.
- `POST /api/wallet` returns the free and paid balances and the welcome outcome. GET is not served.
- `POST /api/cartoonify` gains a session check (401 `AUTH_REQUIRED`), a credit reservation that spends paid credits first (402 `NO_CREDITS`), refunds on every failure after the reservation, and a watermark on free results.
- Schema `protoolhub` with eight tables, RLS enabled on each, and four `SECURITY DEFINER` functions executable by `service_role` only. The migration file was checked by Grep against this description.
- Workshop sign-in panel and credit badge; legal sections `hesap-ve-krediler` on the KVKK and privacy pages.
- Dependencies `@supabase/ssr` 0.12.7, `@supabase/supabase-js` 2.118.0, `sharp` moved to `dependencies`, `@electric-sql/pglite` 0.5.12 in `devDependencies`.

## Welcome credits: operator decision Q6 (2026-10-26)

The operator decided the welcome credits are given once per e-mail address for life. The address is kept only as a SHA-256 hash of the normalised address in `protoolhub.welcome_email_keys`. That table has no link to an account, so deleting an account does not remove the hash, and the same address cannot receive the credits again. Criterion 102 tests this in PGlite. Criteria 88, 90 and 91 also test parts of it (spec Q6).
