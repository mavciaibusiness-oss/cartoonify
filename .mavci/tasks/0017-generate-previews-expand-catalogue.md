# Task 0017 — Style image generation and catalogue expansion to 99 styles

- **Task id:** 0017
- **Phase at writing:** plan

## 1. What this task is

The operator's request: generate the style previews and grow the catalogue to
the 99 active styles of `data/style-catalog-draft.json` (0013).

- **The catalogue.** The 70 new styles are added to `lib/cartoon-styles.ts`
  with their TR and EN names, and the 9 fixed styles get their new prompts.
- **Previews.** Every style is shown on its own subject: woman, man, pet,
  landscape or still life (option A). The assignment is in the table (§3.2).
  - The images are written to `stylePreviewPath(id, 'full')`, then the resize
    script writes `stylePreviewPath(id, 'web')`.
  - No preview path changes in code (0016's rule).
- **Batches.**
  - **Batch 1 is the two resemblance clusters of 0013 §6 and nothing else.**
    Before anything is generated, its prompt list and estimated cost are
    shown to the operator, who approves them first.
  - Its results are shown too, and every later batch starts only after the
    operator has approved the previous batch's results by hash.
- **Money.** The ceiling for the whole task is **$3.50**. If it would be
  exceeded, generation stops and asks.
- **Closing.**
  - The homepage budget (at most 2 600 000 B) is measured again with 99
    styles.
  - Finding 61 is still open, so the verdict and the transfer report are
    shown to the operator.
  - Finding 62: no `next dev` during verify.
  - The closing commands, deploy included, come as one block.

**Decided before this spec was written** (operator, 2026-09-30):
- **The coordinate model is retired (§3.4).**
- **The main session runs the paid batches**, each only after the operator
  has approved it.

**Out of scope:** the "Scenes" catalogue, sign-in and credits, a platform
layer, and the legal pages (§12).

**The count, made precise (the brief asks for it).** "The existing 31" are
29 styles today, since 0014 merged 2.
- 29 + 70 = **99 active styles**.
- **92 are rendered.** Seven are not:
  - `classic`, whose preview is the hero's after-image of `before.jpg`. The
    hero pair must stay true (0008), so it keeps the woman and is not
    re-rendered.
  - Six `keep` styles whose subject is the woman they were already rendered

### 3.2 Subjects (option A)

  or N still life.

- Criterion 58 compares both with this spec.

### 3.3 The 79 written styles

- For the 9 fixed and 70 new styles, this spec writes four things:
  - a TR description and an EN description, in the same register as
    today's, **with no digit** (criterion 15 forbids unexpected numbers on
    the homepage, and the showcase shows descriptions);
  - a closing (`preserve` or `exaggerate`);
  - a TR prompt body inside its length band.
- **Each body carries the draft's `differs_from` point**, the one thing that
  separates the style from its nearest neighbour (0013 §5).
- **Bodies are subject-neutral:** "Bu fotoğrafı …" or "Bu fotoğraftaki
  konuyu …". The portrait-only ones say "Bu portreyi …".
- **Exaggerate** is used by the caricatures and by the styles that change
  proportions: `rubber-hose`, `chibi`, `bobblehead`, `street-caricature`,
  `editorial-cartoon`, `stretched-caricature` and `modelled-caricature`.
  Everything else preserves.
- **Names:**
  - the 70 new styles take `name_tr` and `name_en` from the draft;
  - the fixed styles keep their names (0013 §3.6), but their descriptions
    are rewritten to match their new prompts.
- **Keep styles change nothing but their subject.**
- **Order:** the new styles are appended after today's 29, by category in
  `STYLE_CATEGORIES` order and catalogue order.
  - Today's first two per category therefore stay the showcase samples.
  - `drawing` and `era` get their first two new styles, and `graphic`,
    `surface` and `sculpt` their second card.

**Where the rest goes:**

### 3.5 The batch script (step B)

`scripts/render-batch.mjs` is new; the main session writes it with the
operator's authority, since `scripts/` is outside the builder's scope.
- **Its data** comes from `lib/style-previews.ts`: `PREVIEW_BATCHES`,
  `PREVIEW_SUBJECTS` and `PREVIEW_SOURCES`.
- **Its prompts** come from `getCartoonStyle(id).prompt`.
- **Its paths** come from `stylePreviewPath(id, 'full')`.
- It keeps the request parameters and the safety checks of
  `render-previews.mjs`: the model, size, quality and webp, the stop on no
  usage, the wrong format or the wrong quality, and the record per preview.

**It has five modes:**
1. **`--plan <n>`, free, no provider call.** It prints batch n: each id, its
   subject and source sha256 prefix, and its full prompt. It prints the
   estimated cost (the count × $0.0219) and a **plan hash**: the sha256 of
   `JSON.stringify` of `[id, subject, source sha256, prompt]` for each
   style, in batch order. It writes nothing.
2. **`--render <n> --approved-plan <prefix>`, paid.** It refuses:
   - unless the prefix matches the plan hash, so the prompts cannot have
     changed since the operator saw them;
   - unless every earlier batch has an accepted result whose files still
     hash to it;
   - if the batch itself is already accepted;
   - if a source file no longer hashes to its approved sha256;
   - before any call that would take the batch past **$0.50** or the task
     past **$3.50**. It stops and asks; it never continues.

   It writes each preview and its manifest record: the file, sha256,
   `input_sha256`, subject, `task: '0017'`, `batch`, `plan_sha256`, usage,
   `cost_usd` and time. A replaced record moves to `discarded`, as in
   0008.
3. **`--render <n> --only <id> --approved-plan <prefix>`:** it re-renders
   one style of a batch that is not yet accepted, under the same ceilings.
4. **`--accept <n> --result <prefix>`:** it records the operator's approval
   of batch n's result. The **result hash** is the sha256 of the batch's
   preview sha256s, in batch order. It is stored in the manifest under
   `batches`.
5. **`--status`, free:** each batch's state and spend, and the task total.

**Spend accounting:**
- Only records with `task: '0017'` count toward the $3.50.
- The 0008 spend is history and is not counted.

**What the script is not allowed to do:**
- It never generates a source.
- It reads the key only through `lib/env.ts` (`getEnv()`); the file has no
  `process.env`.
- It uses `images.edit` only, with `maxRetries: 0`, so one command is at
  most one charge per style.

### 3.6 The approval gates, batch by batch

1. The main session runs `--plan 1` and shows the operator the 9 prompts,
   their subjects and the estimated cost ($0.20).
2. **The operator approves** by giving the plan hash prefix.
3. The main session runs `--render 1 --approved-plan <prefix>`. It shows
   the 9 results side by side, grouped by cluster with the cluster's
   existing previews next to them, along with the result hash.
4. **The operator approves the result** by giving that result hash prefix.
   The main session records it with `--accept 1 --result <prefix>`. A weak
   style can be re-rendered with `--only` before acceptance.
5. Batches 2–12 follow the same four steps. The operator may approve
   several batch plans at once, but every result is accepted by its own
   hash before the next batch renders.
6. **After the last acceptance:**
   - `scripts/resize-style-previews.mjs` runs (free) and writes the 99 web
     copies and `lib/style-web-manifest.json`;
   - `STYLE_PREVIEW_IDS` becomes the 99 ids.
