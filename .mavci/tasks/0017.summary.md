## Execution summary

This is a **build task** with three steps. Step A, the builder's, expands the catalogue to 99 active styles, retires the coordinate model, groups the picker by category, and adds subject assignments to `lib/style-previews.ts` (spec §3, §4.1). Step B, the main session's with operator authority, writes two scripts for batch rendering (spec §3.5, §4.2). Step C, the main session's, gated by the operator, renders 92 styles in twelve batches, resizes them, and runs final checks (spec §3.6, §4.5).

**Step A implementation.** The catalogue grows from 29 active styles to 99: 20 are kept with their existing prompts byte-for-byte, 9 are fixed with new prompts and descriptions, and 70 are new with both prompts and descriptions. The coordinate model (`coords`, `asserts`, `deriveGroup`, `closingFor`, and related types) is removed and replaced with an explicit `closing` field (`'preserve'` or `'exaggerate'`) on every style. Every record stores its closing value; the 20 keep styles keep their prompts byte for byte, and every existing style keeps its closing (spec §3.4). The picker is refactored from four coordinate-derived display groups to category-based groups: twelve categories each named in the dictionaries, with non-empty categories only, in `STYLE_CATEGORIES` order. The selection and search remain over these new groups. The category headings are generated from `styleCategories` in `lib/i18n/index.ts` via `groupLabel(id, locale)`. Every style is assigned one of five subjects: K woman, E man, P pet, M landscape, or N still life. The spread is 20 K, 20 E, 20 P, 20 M, 19 N. Resemblance clusters share a subject so their styles compare side by side: cluster 1 (two-ink, double-pass, screen-print, duotone-poster, risograph) is N, and cluster 2 (mass-caricature, chibi, bobblehead, street-caricature) is E (spec §3.2). Subjects and their source images are recorded in `lib/style-previews.ts` as `PREVIEW_SUBJECTS` (99 entries) and `PREVIEW_SOURCES` (five entries with path and approved sha256 prefix). The 70 new styles are appended after the existing 29 in category order. The meta description is updated: `meta.workshopDescription` says 99 styles exactly in both TR and EN (spec §4.1 item 6). All other text in both dictionaries is frozen; descriptions and bodies for the 9 fixed styles are updated; their names remain unchanged (spec §4.1 item 2).

**Step B: two scripts written by the main session.** `scripts/check-styles.mjs` is rewritten to drop coordinate checks and separation rules (the catalogue's `differs_from` field now holds separation notes), add closing-value checks, add `PREVIEW_SUBJECTS` and `PREVIEW_SOURCES` integrity checks, and add `PREVIEW_BATCHES` checks. The new script keeps prompt length band checks, classic's frozen prompt, category rules, and preview parity (spec §3.4). `scripts/render-batch.mjs` is new: it renders styles from `PREVIEW_BATCHES` under two ceilings ($0.50 per batch, $3.50 for the task), with five modes (`--plan`, `--render`, `--only`, `--accept`, `--status`), and requires the operator's approval by hash at every step (plan hash before render, result hash before accept). The script reads the API key through `lib/env.ts`, uses `images.edit` only with `maxRetries: 0`, and has no `process.env` (spec §3.5).

**Step C: 92 renders in 12 batches, gated by the operator.** The six keep styles (`single-ink`, `soft-pastel`, `retro-print`, `three-tone-panel`, `feature-caricature`, `flat-colour`) and `classic` are not re-rendered; their previews are rendered from the woman source (input_sha256 1ff634d7f764…) already in the manifest. The operator approved each batch plan (showing prompts, estimated cost, and plan hash), then the script rendered the batch, the operator approved the result by result hash, and the script recorded acceptance. A 429 "no credits remaining" error from OpenAI occurred after 6 of 8 renders in batch 4; the operator added credits and the same batch completed the remaining 2. Every record is marked with `task: '0017'` and its batch number; old records moved to `discarded`. The operator chose to keep prompts in Turkish, matching the existing styles. After batch 12 was accepted, `scripts/resize-style-previews.mjs` ran (free, no approval needed) and wrote 99 web copies to `public/styles-web/` and `lib/style-web-manifest.json`. Then `STYLE_PREVIEW_IDS` was set to the 99 ids in `CARTOON_STYLES` order.
