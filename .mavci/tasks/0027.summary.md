## What was built

From the spec and the working-tree files:

- `components/intro-video.tsx` (new, `'use client'`): renders `section.intro-video` with `aria-labelledby="intro-video-title"`, the `h2#intro-video-title`, one `<video>` and two controls. The video has `src="/videos/cartoon-intro.mp4"`, `poster="/videos/cartoon-intro-poster.jpg"`, `muted`, `loop`, `playsInline`, `preload="none"`, `width={720} height={1280}`, `aria-label` from `introVideo.videoLabel`, and `controls` only when reduced motion is requested. No `autoplay` attribute.
- Sound button: `button.intro-video-sound`, `type="button"`, `aria-label` is `soundOn` while muted and `soundOff` while sound is on. A click toggles `video.muted` and, if the video is paused, starts it.
- Try button: `Link.intro-video-cta` to `localizedPath(locale, '/workshop')`, `prefetch={false}`, text `featured.cta`. It has its own class, not `featured-cta`, so existing `.featured-cta` selectors still find only the featured band's link (spec D4).
- Playback: an `IntersectionObserver` with `threshold: 0.5` calls `play()` at half visibility and `pause()` otherwise. The observer is disconnected on unmount. When `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` matches, the observer is not created (spec D6, D7).
- `components/landing.tsx`: `<IntroVideo locale={locale} />` is placed after the closing `</section>` of `landing-hero` and before `<FeaturedStyle>`. Both locales get it through the shared `Landing`.
- `lib/i18n/tr.ts` and `lib/i18n/en.ts`: new group `introVideo` with `title`, `soundOn`, `soundOff`, `videoLabel`. Values in tr: `Bir fotoğraf, 120 stil` / `Sesi aç` / `Sesi kapat` / `ProToolHub Cartoon tanıtım videosu`. Values in en: `One photo, 120 styles` / `Turn sound on` / `Turn sound off` / `ProToolHub Cartoon intro video`. (The `videoLabel` values are suggested in the spec; criteria require only a non-empty string.)
- `app/globals.css`: `.intro-video-cta` shares the `.featured-cta` rule (`justify-self: start; font-weight: 700`). New `.intro-video` rules: section box, 9/16 `aspect-ratio` on the player, and from 700 px a two-column body with a 360 px column.
