# Task 0019 — Goofy sketch caricature and a 12-subject pool

- **Task id:** 0019 (allocated by `state.mjs --begin-plan`)
- **Operator's brief:**
  - a new viral style, "Komik Karalama Karikatür" / "Goofy Sketch Caricature" (caricature, exaggerate), that works on one person and on groups, with the background drawn in the same scribble;
  - A/B variants, one tested on a group photo;
  - found by "çirkin", "komik", "karalama", "goofy" and "ugly";
  - the subject pool grows from 5 to 12 (couple, group of friends, family of adults, older woman, bearded man, cat, dog; no real people, celebrities or children), with each subject in at most about 10 styles;
  - one member of each similar pair or trio moves to a new subject;
  - styles whose prompt does not change are only re-rendered on their new subject;
  - the new style is featured on the homepage (before/after with the group photo), with the homepage at most 2 600 000 B;
  - a plan hash and a result hash for every batch, and **$3.00** for the task.
- **Added by the operator before approval:** the landing gallery ("Her fotoğrafa uyan üç stil").
  - It is renewed from the subject pool: at least a group row (with the new style), a couple, a pet, a landscape and a portrait.
  - Each row has 3 of the strongest and most different styles; the operator approved the list (§4.5).
  - Existing previews are reused, and only new subjects are rendered.
  - Criteria 17 and 20 are renewed.
  - The homepage stays at most 2 600 000 B.
  - **The operator's rule for a weak tile:** a gallery style that comes out weak in its batch is replaced by another style on the same subject (a small spec amendment).
- **Operator's decisions** (asked before this spec was written):
  - the featured band goes **under the hero**;
  - the new style gets a **long body band** (300–440 characters, that style only);
  - 0017/0018's provenance criteria are **merged into one integrity criterion**.

## 3. Decisions this spec makes explicitly

### 3.1 The assignment (§4.3)

It was computed by a min-cost flow with these rules (criterion 68 checks the result):
- **Each subject holds 6–10 styles.** Today K20, E22, P21, M17, N19.
- **Within a category, a subject is used at most twice.** Caricature has 9 styles and only 7 people subjects, so strictly "once" is impossible there. In the result only line (M) and caricature (E, G) use a subject twice.
- **People only:**
  - caricatures and the draft's `portrait_only` styles get people subjects (K, E, C, G, A, Y, S);
  - styles whose prompt says "Bu portreyi", plus marble-bust and bobblehead, get a single person (K, E, Y, S).
- **Pairs:** the six similar pairs or trios get distinct subjects, with at least one member on a new subject. From 0018's evidence:
  - **knitted** goes to the cat (T); the knitting on a still life was weak;
  - **sand-art** stays on the landscape (M); its portrait was weak;
  - **mosaic** goes to the dog (D) instead.
- **classic** stays on K (the hero pair), and the new style goes on G.
- **As few moves as possible:** 49, plus the new style. Every move is a re-render with the style's current prompt.

### 3.2 The new style

- Its fields and the two variants are in §4.2.
- It is stored with `band: 'long'`, and check-styles allows that band **for `goofy-sketch` only** (criterion 72).
- **Both variants say:**
  - every person in a group is drawn the same way;
  - the background and objects are scribbled too;
  - it is affectionate, not mocking.
- They name no subject (criterion 68 checks a word list).
- **The test:** A and B are each rendered on the group (G) and on a single man (E). You choose A or B.
- **The preview** is the chosen variant's G render, copied in without another render. The E render is evidence only.
- **Search:** two new exports.
  - `STYLE_KEYWORDS_TR` (`lib/cartoon-styles.ts`): çirkin, komik, karalama.
  - `STYLE_KEYWORDS_EN` (`lib/i18n/styles.en.ts`): goofy, ugly, funny, doodle.
  - `styleMatchesQuery` also searches these words. Descriptions are still not searched (criterion 55, renewed).
  - The words are not shown anywhere.

### 3.3 The featured band

- `components/featured-style.tsx` sits between the hero and the gallery on `/` and `/en`.
- **Contents:**
  - the badge, title and lede;
  - the gallery's group source copy as "before" (`public/gallery/friends/source.webp`, 480 px, q72, written by `--promote` from the accepted G source), so the band and the gallery load it once;
  - the style's web preview as "after";
  - a link to the workshop with `?style=goofy-sketch`.
- **Images:** both are lazy and 480×480. Both are also in the gallery, so the band adds no bytes of its own.
- **Texts:** §4.4 (criterion 71). CSS is token-only, at the end of `app/globals.css`.

### 3.4 The script (step B)

`scripts/render-jobs.mjs` is new and data-driven: `--task 0019`, with `data/render-jobs-0019.json`.
- **What it reuses from 0018's `render-trials.mjs`:** the gates, the records and promotion.
- **What it adds:**
  - **source generation:** `images.generate`, JPEG, no input image, written to `assets/subjects/<name>/source.jpg`;
  - subjects that are themselves results of the same task (a new subject can only be used after its source batch is accepted);
  - the sources' web copies (`webCopies`), which the gallery and the band share.
- **Ceilings:**

- `render-batch.mjs` and `render-trials.mjs` are unchanged.

### 3.5 Batches and approvals

1. **Batch 1: the 7 source photos (`images.generate`).** You may have any one regenerated with `--only src.<K>` before you accept. Accepting fixes each source by its hash.
2. **Batch 2: `goofy-sketch` A and B on G and on E.** You accept with `--choose goofy-sketch=A|B`.
3. **Batches 3–5: the 49 moves, by category (17 + 17 + 15).**
   - `--reject id,…` keeps a style on its old subject and its old preview. The subject totals then move by one, and criterion 68 judges the plan, not the rejection.
   - `--only <id>.S.<K>` re-renders before acceptance.

**Plan hashes:** batch 2–5 plans include the new sources' hashes, so they can be shown only after batch 1 is accepted.

**After the last acceptance:**
1. `--finalize` writes `reports/0019-choices.json`.
2. Step D, the builder: the code (§4.6).
3. `--promote`. It refuses until the code equals the choices. Then it copies the chosen trials into the previews and writes the before-image.
4. The main session adds `goofy-sketch` to `STYLE_PREVIEW_IDS`, runs the resize, and runs `npm run check` and `npm run build`.
